Small shape.
Big personality.

Avatars are everywhere — social profiles, comments, dashboards and messaging apps. Let's understand the CSS behind them.

01

Turn a square image into a circle using the border-radius property.

02

Image cropping

Control how an image fills its container without stretching its proportions.

03

Move the visible image area to find the best portrait composition.
